## What CaseFile is

CaseFile is an AI investigation engine that cites its evidence. You ask a question about a large document collection;
CaseFile searches step by step, follows facts from one document to the next, answers only with what the passages
support, links every claim to its source, reports when sources disagree, and declines when the evidence is missing.
