Postmortem INC-2026-036: authorization timeouts on 11 March 2026

Larkspur Payments (fictional demo data). Severity: SEV-1. Author: Davit Kiknadze. Reviewed by: Mariam Lomidze.

What happened

On 11 March 2026, from 14:40 to 15:35 UTC (55 minutes), many card payments were declined with "authorization timeout". About 1,240 payments were declined; customers had to retry them. Payments that were not declined were processed normally.

Affected services

  • fraud-scoring: response time (p99) rose from 120 ms to 1.9 seconds.
  • payment-gateway: declined payments when fraud-scoring did not answer within its 800 ms limit.

ledger-service, settlement-batch and refund-service were not affected.

Root cause

Change CHG-1214, deployed on 10 March, rolled out fraud model v4. The new model calls the device-reputation lookup for every payment. When afternoon traffic reached its peak, the lookup slowed down, and fraud-scoring answered after the payment-gateway's 800 ms limit. The problem did not show on 10 March because the rollout reached 100% of traffic only on the morning of 11 March.

Resolution

At 15:28 UTC the device-reputation lookup was turned off with a feature flag, which brought fraud-scoring back to the v3 behaviour. Timeouts stopped by 15:35 UTC.

Action items

  1. Cache device-reputation results for 10 minutes before enabling v4 again.
  2. Roll out fraud model changes to 100% of traffic only after a full peak-traffic day at 25%.
