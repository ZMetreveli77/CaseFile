Larkspur Payments on-call runbook (fictional demo data)

Who to page: the on-call engineer for the affected service first. If the incident affects card payments for more
than 15 minutes, page the incident commander on duty as well and open an incident channel.

Severity levels: SEV-1 means card payments are failing for many customers. SEV-2 means card payments are slow or
failing for some customers. SEV-3 means a feature outside the card payment path (payouts, refunds, emails) is broken.

First checks: look at the service dashboard for error rate, response time and queue length. Check the change log
for anything deployed in the last 48 hours; most incidents start after a change. If a recent change is the likely
cause, roll it back first and investigate afterwards.

Writing the report: every incident above SEV-3 needs a written report within 5 working days, with a timeline,
the affected services, the root cause and follow-up actions.
